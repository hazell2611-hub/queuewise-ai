Queue data

✓

Use observed or measured data whenever possible. Hover or tap ? if you are unsure what an input means.

Customer demand
?
What does this mean?

The average number of customers entering the queue in one hour.

How do I get it?

Count customer arrivals during an observed period, then convert the result to customers per hour.

Example

20 customers arriving in 30 minutes is equivalent to approximately 40 customers per hour.

Rp / staff / hour
Service speed
?
What does this mean?

The average time one staff member needs to complete service for one customer.

How do I get it?

Observe several customer transactions and calculate their average service duration.

minutes / customer
Service capacity
?
What should I enter?

Enter the number of staff members or counters that can serve customers at the same time.

Important

Count only active service points involved in this queue.

active service points

    How to read these results

    Average wait

    $
    Cost per day

    How are these results calculated?

    Each scenario is simulated for 30 operating days. Results are reported using the average result and a 95% confidence interval.
